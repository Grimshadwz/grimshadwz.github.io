
                        ////////*********[ ARMED TRUCK COMMANDS LIST]***********///////

/buildbed — Builds the 3-slot armed flatbed onto the modular car you are looking at (requires at least a 3-socket chassis with an empty rear socket and armedflatbed.use permission).

/removebed — Strips the custom flatbed, attached deployables, and saved paged storage/turret data from the modular car you are looking at.

/truckui — Toggles the dashboard CUI control panel while seated inside or looking directly at a modular car within 8 meters.

                        ////////*********[ Custom Wallpaper Tool ]***********///////

/wallpaper — Opens the Custom Wallpaper Tool browser and Workshop import UI.

/wallpaper tool — Gives you the indestructible Custom Wallpaper Tool (skinned Spray Can).

/wallpaper check — Inspects the building block you are looking at (within 6m) and prints wallpaper skin IDs, rotation, health, and zero-decay protection status.

/wallpaper remove <workshopID> — Removes a Workshop wallpaper from the community library, player histories, and any protected surfaces (alias: /wallpaper delete <workshopID>).

/wallpaper debug — Toggles server console debug logging for wallpaper decay healing and vanilla tool overwrites.

/wp [subcommand] — Short alias for /wallpaper (supports all subcommands above).

/wptool — Direct shortcut to receive the Custom Wallpaper Tool.

                        ////////*********[ Heavy Armed Zombies ]***********///////

/setradtown — Adds your current position as a Radtown zone (using the configured RadTownRadius) and immediately spawns a full configured squad of Heavy Armed Zombies there.

/spawnheavyzombie [role] — Spawns a single Heavy Armed Zombie near your location (random role if omitted). Valid <role> arguments:
	ak
	sks
	bolt
	rocket
	grenade
	c4

                        ////////*********[ My Base Companion ]***********///////

/companion — Gives you a deployable Base Companion Post (skinned wooden box) that spawns your personal Base Companion NPC when placed.

                        ////////*********[ Night Lantern ]***********///////

/lantern — Displays the current status of the plugin, whether night mode is active, and the total number of tracked light entities.

/lantern global — (Requires nightlantern.global or Admin) Toggles automatic night lighting on or off across the entire server.

/lantern scan — (Requires nightlantern.global or Admin) Rescans the map for all matching light deployables (Jack O' Lanterns, Tuna Can Lamps, Lanterns, Chinese Lanterns, and Cursed Cauldrons) and updates their state based on the current time of day.

                        ////////*********[ RGB Controller ]***********///////

/rgb — Scans for Red, Green, Blue, and White Industrial Lights within your Tool Cupboard range (50m) and opens the 19-mode RGB Light Controller UI.
